## How Pre-Matching Check-offs Work

Learners always have to attend a pre-matching call.

Volunteers can either attend a pre-matching call or complete self-onboarding instead.

Onboarding calls are booked through an in-app Cal.com pop-up.

At the onboarding appointment, attendance must be checked and users must be marked as attended or unattended afterwards.

## Process

1. Visit [`https://little-world.com/matching/prematch-appointments/`](https://little-world.com/matching/prematch-appointments/).
2. Select the correct pre-matching appointment date/time.
3. Check the boxes for users who attended.
4. Confirm to send follow-up emails and automatically unlock attended users for matching.

## Important warnings

- ⚠️ **Don't check off more than once. This will trigger additional emails.**
- ⚠️ **Avoid checking off manually on user details. If you do, verify whether you need extra manual steps (for example, granting random calls access).**
- ⚠️ **Avoid bypassing this process in any way. If someone lost account access, use password change or email reset flows instead.**
